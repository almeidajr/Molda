# Molda

Molda supports sewing pattern drafting work. This glossary records the shared product vocabulary.

## Language

**Pattern calculation assistant**:
A tool that helps a sewist calculate dimensions used when drafting a sewing pattern from body measurements and explicit drafting assumptions.
_Avoid_: Automatic pattern generator

**Pattern draft (molde)**:
A calculation for one garment that turns body measurements and drafting assumptions into the dimensions needed to draft that garment's pattern.
_Avoid_: Calculator, garment, peça

**Guide (guia)**:
Step-by-step instruction that teaches one sewing technique without calculating anything itself; any pattern draft may refer to it.
_Avoid_: Tutorial as a separate concept

**Tool (ferramenta)**:
A utility that works on a pattern draft's calculated result, such as checking whether the pattern fits the fabric.
_Avoid_: Feature, utility screen

**Project (projeto)**:
A named, saved pattern draft that keeps the measurements, drafting assumptions and result used when it was saved, even if the measurement profile they came from later changes or is deleted.
_Avoid_: Saved calculation, saved molde

**Full-circle skirt (saia godê inteira)**:
A skirt whose main body is drafted from a complete circle with an opening for the waist.

**Fabric folding**:
Arranging fabric in layers before marking or cutting a sewing pattern.
_Avoid_: Pleating

**Pleat**:
A fold formed in a garment's fabric to control fullness or create shape.
_Avoid_: Fabric folding before cutting

**Measurement profile**:
A named collection of body measurements for one person, recorded on a measurement date and optionally accompanied by notes, for reuse in drafting calculations.
_Avoid_: User account, client account

**Length**:
A finite, non-negative distance in centimetres, used for both body measurements and drafting assumptions.

**Drafting assumption**:
A value the sewist chooses rather than measures on the body, such as waist ease, seam and hem allowances, or finished skirt length.
_Avoid_: Measurement

**Waist ease (folga de cintura)**:
The amount added to the person's waist circumference to obtain the intended waist circumference of the skirt at the stitching line.
_Avoid_: Seam allowance

**Seam allowance (margem de costura)**:
Fabric retained beyond a stitching line to join garment pieces.
_Avoid_: Ease

**Hem allowance (margem de barra)**:
Fabric retained beyond the intended finished hemline to finish the hem.

**Finished skirt length**:
The distance from the skirt's waist stitching line to its finished hemline, excluding waistband height and allowances.

**Release**:
The public launch of a version of Molda, allowed only after its release gates pass, such as a practical sewist trial.
_Avoid_: Beta build

**Beta build**:
A version of Molda distributed to known testers before the release it leads up to, so they can use it and report what they observe.
_Avoid_: Release, trial build
