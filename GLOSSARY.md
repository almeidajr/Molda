# Molda

Molda supports sewing pattern drafting work. This glossary records the shared product vocabulary.

## Language

**Pattern calculation assistant**:
A tool that helps a sewist calculate dimensions used when drafting a sewing pattern from body measurements and explicit drafting assumptions.
_Avoid_: Automatic pattern generator

**Full-circle skirt (saia godê inteira)**:
A skirt whose main body is drafted from a complete circle with an opening for the waist.

**Fabric folding**:
Arranging fabric in layers before marking or cutting a sewing pattern.
_Avoid_: Pleating

**Pleat**:
A fold formed in a garment's fabric to control fullness or create shape.
_Avoid_: Fabric folding before cutting

**Measurement profile**:
A named collection of body measurements for one person, recorded on a measurement date and optionally accompanied by notes, for reuse in drafting calculations.
_Avoid_: User account, client account

**Waist ease (folga de cintura)**:
The amount added to the person's waist circumference to obtain the intended waist circumference of the skirt at the stitching line.
_Avoid_: Seam allowance

**Seam allowance (margem de costura)**:
Fabric retained beyond a stitching line to join garment pieces.
_Avoid_: Ease

**Hem allowance (margem de barra)**:
Fabric retained beyond the intended finished hemline to finish the hem.

**Finished skirt length**:
The distance from the skirt's waist stitching line to its finished hemline, excluding waistband height and allowances.
